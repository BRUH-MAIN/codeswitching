   \textbf{Environment split.} Phases 1 and 2 are separate notebooks by necessity:
   \texttt{parler-tts} pins \texttt{transformers==4.46.1} while Gemma\,4 needs
   $\ge$\,5.5 --- they cannot share a process, so the Hub is the checkpoint between them.
   Every stage reads a manifest and writes a manifest; no stage passes a live object to another.\\[2pt]
   \textbf{Key deviations from the paper.}
   \textbf{D1} LLM 70B $\rightarrow$ 26B-A4B MoE $\cdot$
   \textbf{D2/D15} large-v2 $\rightarrow$ whisper-base, 5000 $\rightarrow$ 800 steps (primary confound) $\cdot$
   \textbf{D4/D8} WhisperX $\rightarrow$ faster-whisper, recording-level $\cdot$
   \textbf{D14} insertional $\rightarrow$ alternational code-switching.
